Security Architecture

How AVFA Validates Academic Truth

A military-grade 4-step cryptographic and neural inspection pipeline designed to prevent forgery across digital and physical certificates.

01

Canonical Hashing

Student name, roll number, course, marks, and CGPA are mapped into a standardized byte payload and hashed via deterministic SHA-256.

02

RSA-2048 Signing

The issuing institution signs the cryptographic hash using its certified private key, making the document mathematically unforgeable.

03

Multi-Modal Extraction

When a document is uploaded, PyMuPDF extractors and EasyOCR parse glyphs and ReportLab tables to identify any modified marks or CGPA.

04

Instant Truth Decision

The digital signature is checked against the institutional public key. Any mismatch between document values and registered record immediately flags as TAMPERED.
